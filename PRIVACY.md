# Privacy policy

Gymneat. Effective 22 November 2026.

Gymneat is a gym tracker made by serrant. This policy covers the Android app and the web version.

## The short version

Gymneat does not collect, send or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

## What the app stores

Your training plan, your own exercises, the sessions and sets you log, and your body weight entries. They are saved only on your device: on Android in the app's private storage, on the web in your browser. They are never sent to the developer or to anyone else.

## What the app does not do

- It has no server and no account, and never asks for your name, email or other personal details.
- It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party services.
- The Android app has no internet permission, so it cannot connect to the internet at all.

## Permissions on Android

- Notifications: to tell you when a rest ends while you are in another app.
- Alarms and reminders (optional): so that notification arrives on time.
- Run at startup and keep the phone awake: so a scheduled rest notification still arrives after a restart or while the phone sleeps.
- Vibration: to vibrate when a rest ends.

All of them work only on your device.

## When data leaves your device

Only when you choose to. Export saves a backup file and opens the share menu, so you decide where it goes. Copy JSON copies your data to the clipboard.

If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include the app's data in your own Google account backup. Google handles that backup under its own privacy policy.

The web version is downloaded from the site that hosts it, which may keep standard server logs such as your IP address. The app itself sends nothing.

## Deleting your data

On Android, uninstall the app, or go to Settings > Apps > Gymneat > Storage > Clear storage. On the web, clear the site's data in your browser. The developer never receives your data, so there is nothing to delete on the developer's side.

## Children

Gymneat collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
